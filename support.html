Support

Help with AnglerBook · GLYSK OÜ

Need a hand with AnglerBook? The fastest way to reach us is email — write to development@glysk.eu and we’ll normally reply within 2–3 business days. To help us help you, include your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

AnglerBook Pro & billing

AnglerBook Pro comes in four options: a one-time 3-day pass (€1.99) or 7-day pass (€4.99), and auto-renewing Monthly (€7.99) or Annual (€29.99) plans. All are sold and billed by Apple through your App Store account — not by GLYSK directly. Monthly and Annual renew until you cancel them under Settings → your name → Subscriptions; the passes are one-time and simply expire. Refunds are handled by Apple. If you’ve paid but Pro isn’t showing, open AnglerBook and use Restore Purchases in the app’s settings while signed in with the same Apple ID.

iCloud sync

If your log isn’t appearing across devices, check that each device is signed into the same Apple ID, that iCloud Drive is on, and that AnglerBook is allowed to use iCloud under Settings → your name → iCloud. Sync runs in the background and can take a moment on a slow connection.

Your data & privacy

Your fishing journal lives on your device and, if you turn backup on, in your own iCloud account or your own Google Drive — GLYSK never receives a copy of your catches, photos or coordinates. Two GLYSK services do receive data: anonymous session statistics that carry no identifier, and the opt-in Fishing Buddies layer (your nickname, connections and standings). The weather provider receives the session's coordinates in order to return the forecast for that spot. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy. For any data question or request — including deleting your Fishing Buddies record — email development@glysk.eu.

Permissions (location, photos, Health)

AnglerBook only uses the permissions you grant, and only to provide the features you use — GPS to place a session, your photo library to attach catch and fly photos, and Apple Health to read activity such as calories. You can change or withdraw any of these at any time under iOS Settings → AnglerBook.

Beta testing

Running a TestFlight build? Please send feedback straight from TestFlight (screenshots welcome) or email us. Beta builds are pre-release, so keep anything important backed up.

Still stuck?

Email development@glysk.eu with a short description and we’ll take it from there. Support is currently offered in English.

Apple is not responsible for AnglerBook or its support. Where subscription billing is handled by Apple, Apple’s own support and policies also apply.
